DB-01

System-on-chip based on cores comprising the Universal Multifunction Accelerator (UMA), positioned for baseband processing and acceleration of vision and AI algorithms.

A practical system-level implementation of the UMA computing architecture.

What the public material supports

DB-01 is described as a system-on-chip based on high-performance computing IP cores built around UMA. Its simple interfaces are positioned for baseband processing and acceleration of vision and artificial-intelligence algorithms.

Applications

  • NAVIC / IRNSS based positioning systems
  • Radar and communication workloads
  • Vision and AI workload acceleration

Software enablement

The official product material states that a performance-optimized software SDK for vision and AI processing is available for UMA IP, intended to enable rapid development of new software and porting of existing algorithms.
