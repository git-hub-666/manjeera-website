Applications

Where the architecture fits.

From cloud and edge acceleration to node computing, autonomous systems, vision and signal processing, the application story follows the workloads UMA is designed to address.

01 / CLOUD & DATA CENTER

Highly parallel workloads

Acceleration of frequently used, highly parallel and highly vectorized workloads, including machine learning/deep learning training and inference and streaming data processing.

02 / EDGE COMPUTING

Compute at the point of collection

AI/ML acceleration at the edge can reduce latency and transmission costs. The public application material gives predictive maintenance as an example, including preliminary screening and real-time analysis.

03 / NODE COMPUTING

Portable & embedded systems

AI/ML imaging workloads on handheld, portable and embedded devices where low power matters. Examples include object detection, classification, identification, AVMR devices, dialog systems and surveillance.

04 / SIGNAL PROCESSING

DSP, baseband & radar

Acceleration of DSP algorithms, including NavIC baseband processing for IRNSS-based positioning receiver devices and Synthetic Aperture Radar workloads.
