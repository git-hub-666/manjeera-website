Careers

Work on intelligent computing.

Manjeera is looking for enthusiastic, innovative and motivated people across semiconductor design, verification, DSP, AI and embedded software.

OPEN TO

Fresh graduates & experienced engineers.

The public careers page states that candidates with up to six years of experience and candidates with Master’s or higher degrees in relevant subjects can apply.

HARDWARE

RTL Design

Verilog/SystemVerilog, front-end design and verification flows, IP/SoC development, FPGA prototyping and awareness of AMBA, PCIe, DDR and VLSI flows.

HARDWARE

RTL Verification

Digital electronics, VLSI flow, SystemVerilog/C test cases and testbenches, coverage, ASIC simulation and verification methodology.

SOFTWARE

DSP / AI / Embedded

DSP, neural networks, C/Matlab/Python, embedded systems, AI models and frameworks including TensorFlow, PyTorch, ONNX and OpenVINO.
