Manjeera Digital Systems is a Hyderabad-based technology company focused on advanced computing architectures and hardware IP.

About Manjeera

Performance, flexibility and efficiency.

The company develops computing architectures and hardware IP to address limitations in conventional processor, co-processor and accelerator approaches.

At the heart of this work is UMA, based on Middle Stratum Operations (MSO), a programmable architecture designed to combine high performance with power efficiency.

Applications include edge computing, computer vision, signal processing, AI/ML and embedded systems.
