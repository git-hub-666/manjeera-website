Company / About

Designed around the workload.

Manjeera Digital Systems is a Hyderabad-based technology company focused on next-generation computing architectures and high-performance digital systems.

ABOUT MANJEERA
Addressing the bottlenecks of conventional processor, co-processor and accelerator architectures.

Manjeera Digital Systems develops computing architectures and hardware IP to address the demands of high-performance computing, AI, vision, signal processing and embedded systems.

The company’s architecture is based on Middle Stratum Operations (MSO), with UMA designed to combine high performance with power efficiency and programmability.
