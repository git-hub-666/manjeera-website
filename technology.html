Technology & Validation

UMA: hardware acceleration with software flexibility.

Universal Multifunction Accelerator is Manjeera’s computing architecture and hardware IP based on Middle Stratum Operations (MSO).

UMA

Universal Multifunction Accelerator

UMA is designed to combine the performance advantages of hardware acceleration with the flexibility of software. It supports configurable, parallel computation for machine learning, deep learning, CNNs, RNNs, computer vision, image processing and signal processing.
